Bottom line

Stress-test verdict: no v2 performance boost was demonstratedAcross baseline, renamed, unseen-predicate and external-KG ranking, v2 only ties v1. On 100 adversarial frontiers where v1's endpoint-overlap heuristic is deliberately wrong, both v1 and v2 score 0% top-1. The learned model has reproduced the training distribution but has not learned a stronger general search policy.

What the evidence supportsThe optimizer is stable, the in-template task is learned, latency passes, and moderate label noise does not immediately destroy ranking on the same easy template distribution.
Engineering decisionKeep v1 as the default. The present mean-token MLP cannot distinguish graph roles reliably after vocabulary shift and does not repair the frontiers v1 gets wrong. A real v2 improvement requires role-aware/graph structural features and new training traces containing actual v1 mistakes.
